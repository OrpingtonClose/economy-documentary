### Stylistic Guidelines (User Feedback - 2026-03-29)

**Tone to avoid:**
- Smart-assy / clever rhetorical questions ("So if that’s true, why doesn’t the Fed just print its way out of this?")
- Forcing every element into a single clever narrative thread
- Overly performative or "gotcha" narrative style

**Preferred approach:**
- **Disaggregate** particular events and mechanisms
- Analyze each element in a **clever, inventive, and incisive** way
- Clinical, sharp, segmented analytical style
- Let the contradictions and mechanics speak for themselves rather than narrating them with attitude

This guidance should inform all future scene writing, claim analysis, and narrative framing.
