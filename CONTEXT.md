### Stylistic Guidelines

**Tone to avoid:**
- Smart-assy / clever rhetorical questions (e.g. "So if that's true, why doesn't X just do Y?")
- Forcing every element into a single clever narrative thread
- Overly performative or "gotcha" narrative style
- Anxiety-spiraling or doom-scrolling energy
- Lecture mode — talking AT the viewer instead of exploring WITH them

**Preferred approach:**
- **Disaggregate** particular events and mechanisms
- Analyze each element in a **clever, inventive, and incisive** way
- Clinical, sharp, segmented analytical style
- Let the contradictions and mechanics speak for themselves rather than narrating them with attitude
- Use the 3-voice system (Hook / Expert / Storyteller) per scene
- Keep scenes under 45 seconds — ADHD-friendly pacing

**ADHD Principles:**
- Max 45s per scene before a tonal or visual shift
- Visual variety: no two consecutive scenes with the same camera angle/environment
- Dopamine hooks every ~15s (surprising fact, visual shift, voice change)
- Never use rhetorical questions as filler — every question must be immediately answered

This guidance should inform all future scene writing, claim analysis, and narrative framing
regardless of the documentary topic.
