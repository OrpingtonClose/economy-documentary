Legal does not mean uninformed. The question the STOCK Act was written to prevent was: did you trade because you knew something the market didn't? The question it was not written to answer is: what does it do to the incentive structure of national security decision-making when the people voting on war have a financial stake in war's outcome? If a vote for military action will increase the value of your portfolio, and a vote against it will not, is the vote unaffected? The STOCK Act does not think this question is relevant. Behavioral economics disagrees.
