[V3] This is the structural problem with economic warfare as a substitute for diplomacy. Sanctions compress the formal economy — they make dollar-clearing, SWIFT transfers, and legitimate trade harder. They do not compress the informal economy with the same efficiency. And modern finance is sufficiently sophisticated that the gap between formal and informal is navigable by anyone with enough capital and enough lawyers. Iran had both.
