The Breakwave Tanker Shipping ETF — BWET — was up ninety-eight percent year-to-date as of February twenty-eighth. Ninety-eight percent in less than two months, in a fund that tracks operational shipping companies. Gold ETF inflows hit nineteen billion dollars globally — a record. These numbers, individually, could each be explained by sophisticated macro analysis. Together, they describe something specific and consistent: a market that had already processed the probability of a major Persian Gulf conflict and was expressing it in its position book.
