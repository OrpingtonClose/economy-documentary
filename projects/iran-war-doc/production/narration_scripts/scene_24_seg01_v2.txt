But the pre-war baseline was already fragile, which is the detail that makes the Goldman call serious. GDP in Q4 2025 had been revised down to zero-point-seven percent annualized growth — half the previous estimate of one-point-three percent. The revision came from CNN's reporting on March thirteenth, citing Bureau of Economic Analysis data. Zero-point-seven percent is not a recession, technically. It is not growth that can absorb an external shock. An economy growing at zero-point-seven percent is an economy with no margin. The war provided the shock. The margin was not there.
