On the same timeline: five-point-eight million Brent crude call options were purchased, clustered at strike prices of eighty-five, ninety, and one hundred dollars per barrel. Each of those strikes was above the prevailing market price at the time of purchase. They were bets that oil would go significantly higher — not marginally, but to specific thresholds. They paid off within forty-eight hours of the first strike. In option terms, they went from out-of-the-money to deeply in-the-money in one weekend. The profit on a five-point-eight million contract position moving through those strikes is measured in the billions.
