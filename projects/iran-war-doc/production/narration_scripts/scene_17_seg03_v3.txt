Naomi Klein, in 'The Shock Doctrine,' argued that crisis is an opportunity for capital to restructure in its own favor while attention is elsewhere. She was writing about neoliberal economic reform in post-crisis economies. She was also, unknowingly, describing this. Every war is a redistribution event. The question is always: redistribution toward whom? The answer, in this war as in most, runs consistently upward. The war destroys the middle. The wealth survives in the shell company.
