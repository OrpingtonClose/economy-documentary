The Strategic Petroleum Reserve release — one hundred seventy-two million barrels, the largest coordinated IEA release in history, across thirty-two countries releasing a combined four hundred million barrels — was designed to blunt this. It worked. Partially. For a while. But every barrel released from the SPR is a barrel that will cost approximately one hundred dollars or more to replace. That is not a solved problem. That is a deferred bill. Deferred bills do not disappear. They compound. The deferred refill cost of the SPR release is approximately twenty billion dollars, payable at a future date, by a future taxpayer, for a decision made in March 2026.
