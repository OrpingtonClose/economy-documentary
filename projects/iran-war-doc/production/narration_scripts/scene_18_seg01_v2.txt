Specific commitments. THAAD production: from ninety-six interceptors per year to four hundred per year — more than a four-fold increase, requiring new production lines, new workforce, new supply chain capacity. Tomahawk cruise missile production: to exceed one thousand per year, from a base that had already been running below demand. These are not incremental adjustments. These are multi-year industrial transformation orders. They require capital investment. They require facility expansion. They require long-term supply chain contracts with hundreds of sub-tier suppliers. They require, above all, long-term contract certainty from the government — the kind that generates predictable revenue streams and predictable margins for five, ten, fifteen years.
