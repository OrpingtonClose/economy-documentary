Bloomberg's March eighth investigation — 'How the Son of Iran's Supreme Leader Built a Global Property Empire' — documents the full scope. The Hilton Frankfurt Gravenbruch, a five-star hotel in Germany's financial capital. Hotels in Mallorca. A villa in Dubai's most exclusive residential district — described in real estate marketing as 'the Beverly Hills of Dubai.' A C$10.5 million penthouse in Toronto, sold in 2020 — after the sale, but before the war. And the centerpiece: over one hundred million pounds in UK property holdings, concentrated on The Bishops Avenue, a ten-minute drive from the Israeli embassy in London.
