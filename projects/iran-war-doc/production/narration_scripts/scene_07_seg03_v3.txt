The Romans called it 'pecunia non olet' — money does not smell. It does not carry the scent of the decision that produced it. A stock gain from a war you voted for looks exactly like a stock gain from a war you voted against. The ledger records the profit. The ledger does not record the mechanism. And that is precisely the point: we have constructed a system in which the financial consequences of a decision are legible, and the decision's relationship to those consequences is deliberately obscured.
