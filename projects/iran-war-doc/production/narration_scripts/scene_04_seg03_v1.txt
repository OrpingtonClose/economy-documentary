Here is the question that drives this entire documentary. Not: was it worth it? That is a political question, and politics will outlast the answer. The real question is: who decided it was affordable? Who was in the room? Who had their financial positions arranged to benefit from the answer being yes? Because somebody knew. And the paper trail — in the CFTC filings, in the tanker ETF returns, in the congressional disclosure forms — says they knew eight weeks in advance. That story begins in Act Two.
