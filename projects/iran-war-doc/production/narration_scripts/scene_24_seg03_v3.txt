The economist Hyman Minsky observed that stability breeds instability — that the longer an economy is stable, the more leverage accumulates, and the more fragile it becomes to disruption. The Q4 2025 GDP revision suggests an economy that had been leveraged into fragility before the war. The war was the disruption. The Goldman call was the market's assessment that fragility plus disruption equals twenty-five percent probability of the thing that hurts ordinary people most: a recession.
