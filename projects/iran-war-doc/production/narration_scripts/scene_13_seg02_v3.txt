The Trump administration's response — framed through Secretary Bessent — was to invoke market stability and economic sovereignty. 'We are a market-driven economy. This is a market-driven decision.' It was a response that treated a geopolitical alliance like a vendor contract. Deliverable when convenient, renegotiable under price pressure. The Europeans had no legal recourse. No enforcement mechanism. The Atlantic alliance is not a binding contract with penalties for breach. It is a shared commitment. And shared commitments are only as durable as the perception of shared interest.
