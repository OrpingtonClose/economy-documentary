Let us be precise about what this means and does not mean. This is not a claim that Binance intentionally funded the IRGC. It is a claim that the regulatory architecture for large-scale stablecoin flows was insufficient to prevent it. It is a claim that the gap between the legal standard — know your customer, anti-money laundering, OFAC screening — and the operational reality of a high-volume global crypto exchange processing billions of transactions per day was wide enough to drive one-point-seven billion dollars through. It is not a conspiracy claim. It is a systems claim.
